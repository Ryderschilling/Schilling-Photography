The Experience

You Will Feel Welcomed, Cherished, and Cared For

01

Initial Consultation

We chat with you by phone or in person. We love to hear your story: how you met, the vision for your day, and the style of photography you are drawn to. We walk through pricing and services with zero pressure.

02

Booking

Once you decide Schilling Photography is the perfect fit, booking is simple and fully online, with a deposit to lock your date. Every new client gets a welcome box from us.

03

Planning + Preparation

As your wedding gets closer, we walk through every detail together: engagement session, wedding day timeline, location scouting by season, vendor recommendations, and photography tips so you feel completely ready.

04

Wedding Day + Delivery

On the day, we are there to create and document your most precious moments while you stay present in them. Fully edited images arrive fast, with easy viewing and download access, and we are still here for family photos and anniversaries after.
