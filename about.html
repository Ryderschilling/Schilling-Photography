About Us

Hi!! We Jarod and Julia Schilling! We are a family based photography team…starting from the top- Jarod's dad started the business over 40 years ago! We love photography! And we love our business! Families, weddings, puppies, babies, friendships, any and all we love it, and we love to get behind the lens and capture those moments in time that should never be forgotten! Life is precious. Days are short. But beautiful it is!! We feel beyond blessed to have the gift of each day, and our goal is to live this life to the fullest! We have over 20 years of experience and we just LOVE what we do!!

We specialize in wedding and lifestyle (YES we do portraits of seniors, babies, families, pets, all of it) photography!! We love getting to know YOU!

We are a super tight, close knit family. We love the outdoors, sports, fishing, vacations (Julia is a Travel Planner!!), DISNEY, and we love our friends and family, and have a deep rooted faith. We love to laugh, are all about good times, and love to get to the heart of the matter as well. Just enjoying this journey called life together!! We are blessed with an INCREDIBLE calling- to photograph and document the most precious moments in life!!

The thing is…we met in second grade and have loved each other ever since!!

We have weathered the good times and the rough times together, and we just adore this life we have together!!! We truly are best friends! We have been married over twenty years! And our business has been a part of our life since the beginning of our marriage! Julia has recently become a Travel Planner! Specializing in Luxury & Adventure Travel- can you say honeymoons!?? And also Disney Travel! We love that our businesses can go hand in hand…as we have all through the years!!

We are SO blessed to be their mom and dad. Our life revolves around our family, and we strive to keep a very close relationship with our children!! This is truly where the REAL treasure is in this life!! Family is everything!! Olive LOVES photography, has recently started driving, is amazing musically, and just overall a beautiful young woman inside and out!!
